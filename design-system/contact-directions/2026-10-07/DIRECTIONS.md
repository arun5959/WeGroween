# Contact page — structure directions

PROPOSED. Existing website unchanged.

Recommended: **The Harvest Desk**. It gives the confirmed email and phone priority, then helps the visitor provide useful enquiry context.

## 1. The Harvest Desk

A calm editorial opening with a clear contact panel; enquiry topics sit underneath.

Benefit: The fastest route to the confirmed email and phone number. A compact topic guide helps visitors explain their needs.

Tradeoff: Every enquiry starts through email or phone; this does not create a website message inbox.

1. Split introduction and direct contact panel
2. Three equal enquiry-topic choices with shared guidance
3. Existing brand footer

Mobile: Heading, contact panel, topic choices and guidance stack in that priority.

Motion: 140ms topic-selection colour feedback; no hero animation.

## 2. A Letter to WeGroween

A structured email draft gives a visitor a little help putting their question into words.

Benefit: Best when messages need context, especially produce preferences and restaurant quantities.

Tradeoff: More effort than a direct contact page. This preview prepares an email draft; a native send form would need a separately chosen delivery service.

1. Editorial introduction
2. Four-field email draft with direct contact details beside it
3. Existing brand footer

Mobile: Direct email and phone remain visible above the form; fields stack vertically.

Motion: 140ms focus and error feedback; draft region appears without movement.

## 3. The Kitchen Guide

A short, useful question guide sits alongside direct contact, with an enquiry action for each topic.

Benefit: Helps visitors understand what information to include before contacting the business.

Tradeoff: Requires more content discipline; keep answers specific and avoid becoming a long FAQ page.

1. Editorial introduction
2. Three question accordions beside direct contact
3. Existing brand footer

Mobile: Direct contact first, then the three questions.

Motion: Native details expansion; no animated height or scroll hijacking.

All use the same contact facts, introduction, enquiry topics, approved palette, typography, buttons, icons and shared footer. Comparing page architecture, not palette alternatives. Existing /help route is retained; Contact is a proposed navigation label, pending selection.

Evidence: user-supplied email, phone and Gohana; Instagram from the legacy site; current catalogue categories and routes verified from source. There are no confirmed hours, response times, exact farm address or live delivery coverage, so none are invented. No new imagery is needed for a contact-first page.

Interaction: gallery selection; enquiry-topic guidance and email subjects; local draft validation and mailto generation; native FAQ accordions; keyboard menu. No requests submit a message. The gallery stores no form data.

Decision needed: select, combine or revise the three structures. A native website form would require a separate delivery-service decision after structure selection.
