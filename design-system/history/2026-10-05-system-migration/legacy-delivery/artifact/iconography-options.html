Shared rules

Recognition before expression.

Whatever direction is selected, functional clarity remains non-negotiable.

  1. 01
    Use labels when meaning is not universal.

    Crop, harvest, restaurant and cultivation icons reinforce text; they do not replace it.

  2. 02
    Keep controls at least 44×44px.

    An icon may be 20px while its interactive target remains comfortably usable.

  3. 03
    Use currentColor.

    Icons inherit semantic foreground colors and never encode status through color alone.

  4. 04
    Do not mix external families casually.

    Missing glyphs must be drawn to the selected construction rules or explicitly documented as exceptions.

  5. 05
    Avoid logo behavior.

    Icons identify actions and categories. They do not become a substitute monogram or unofficial brand mark.
