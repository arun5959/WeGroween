# Our Story — visual variety concepts

PROPOSED. Production website is unchanged.

## Grower's Field Notes — recommended

A considered mix of crop photographs, small annotations, purpose diagrams and the brand mark. Each chapter gets a visual role of its own.

Benefit: The broadest variety while preserving the warm editorial identity; can evolve as genuine origin photographs become available.

Tradeoff: Needs authentic origin imagery to fully express the first chapter. Current crop photographs illustrate subject matter, not historical events.

1. A genuine terrace photograph, framed with one quiet crop note
2. Paired microgreen and mushroom photographs with crop labels
3. A simple diagram connecting the crops to a kitchen
4. The supplied logo presented as the moment the idea gained a name
5. A produce composition leading towards the catalogue

Motion: 140ms state feedback; 360ms one-time photo-frame and annotation reveal; no image zoom or continuous floating.

Mobile: Photographs remain side by side where readable, with labels below; diagrams and brand frames reflow above their chapter copy.

## Living Cultivation

Five bespoke scenes derived from Field Notes linework: planters, growing trays, a produce-to-kitchen connection, the brand and a table setting.

Benefit: A distinctive illustrated story with consistent craft and no dependency on origin photography.

Tradeoff: Creates emotional meaning, but cannot provide documentary proof of the growing operation. Bespoke illustration requires more refinement than enlarging icons.

1. Terrace planters and a climbing stem
2. A window, microgreen tray and mushroom cluster
3. Crops and basket connected to a kitchen
4. The supplied brand mark framed by cultivation linework
5. A table setting with crop details

Motion: 360ms line drawing as each scene enters; the timeline connects the scenes. No looping growth animation.

Mobile: Whole scenes scale to their viewBox; labels remain HTML text. Important content never depends on animation.

## Harvest Editorial

A photographic reading rhythm: an establishing image, a close detail, a composed still life, a brand interlude and a table scene.

Benefit: The most sensory and food-focused direction, using changing image geometry and crop scale rather than more decoration.

Tradeoff: A consistently art-directed photo set is needed. Produce portraits alone cannot tell the founder's personal history.

1. A full terrace scene from the actual origin garden
2. One generous crop detail with a secondary mushroom inset
3. A wide produce still life
4. A quiet branded interlude
5. A wide table photograph or current supplied produce composition

Motion: 360ms restrained opacity reveal; photographs stay still. Gentle chapter transition, no parallax or scroll locking.

Mobile: Replace extreme detail crops with wider focal framing so the crops remain recognizable; keep text outside photography.

Imagery provenance: Existing public/media files are supplied source assets. Their capture/generation origin is not verified here. They are subject-matter illustrations, not cultivation or founding evidence.

No terrace or founder origin photographs were supplied in the current media inventory. Their slots remain visibly reserved in this review.
