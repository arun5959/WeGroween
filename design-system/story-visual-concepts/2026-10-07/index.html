Why it fits

The most sensory and food-focused direction, using changing image geometry and crop scale rather than more decoration.

What it needs

A consistently art-directed photo set is needed. Produce portraits alone cannot tell the founder's personal history.

Motion and mobile

360ms restrained opacity reveal; photographs stay still. Gentle chapter transition, no parallax or scroll locking. Replace extreme detail crops with wider focal framing so the crops remain recognizable; keep text outside photography.
