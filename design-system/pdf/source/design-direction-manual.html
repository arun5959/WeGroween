01 - Executive direction

The approved system.

Premium is demonstrated through operational truth, sensory specificity and disciplined editorial hierarchy - not generic luxury language.

Positioning

WeGroween is a premium, harvest-aware food brand making microgreens, mushrooms, organic fruit and vegetables, and unusual farm products easier to trust and buy for the home. Restaurant supply is a visible secondary path.

Approved

Cultivated Editorial

A refined food journal with the clarity and state discipline of dependable commerce.

  • Emotional objective: cultivated confidence
  • Visual behavior: warm, tactile, precise
  • Commerce behavior: explicit and efficient

02 - Approved brief

Business, audience and conversion context.

Households lead the experience. Professional kitchens receive a deliberate service path without turning the brand into a wholesale portal.

Business context

  • Products: microgreens, mushrooms, organic fruit and vegetables, and exotic farm products
  • Model: premium household commerce with restaurant supply available
  • Brand name meaning: WeGroween combines "we grow green"
  • Future commerce context: online ordering, admin order handling, Supabase and Razorpay

Primary conversion

  • Browse what is actually ready
  • Check serviceability early
  • Understand pack size, timing and use
  • Add to basket or reserve a harvest
  • Contact restaurant supply with meaningful requirements
Primary audience

Quality-conscious households who want distinctive produce, credible freshness and cooking relevance.

Secondary audience

Restaurants, cafes and chefs who need availability, pack size, supply continuity and human support.

Future operators

Staff who require accurate products, claims, inventory, orders, delivery and customer information.

03 - Research synthesis

Patterns were researched, not pages to copy.

Market evidence, current project assets, accessibility guidance and local resources were normalized with source, access date, confidence and limitation fields.

Research method

  • Audited the current WeGroween static catalogue and assets
  • Reviewed current direct and adjacent food-commerce references
  • Examined accessibility, food-commerce and implementation guidance
  • Inspected approved local visual resources on the E: drive
  • Separated observed facts, interpretation and original hypotheses

Evidence limits

  • No production analytics or customer interviews
  • Service areas, slot rules and operating capacity remain unknown
  • Product catalogue, pack data and claims are not final
  • Local stock resources have uncertain reuse rights
  • Compliance requires qualified legal and accounting review
Trust is operational

Source, availability, delivery, substitutions, refunds and support create more confidence than badges alone.

Freshness needs states

Harvest dates and next availability convert biological variability into useful expectations.

Repeat shopping matters

Buy-again and saved lists should reduce effort when the application phase begins.

Perishables reshape checkout

Serviceability, slots and stock risk must appear before payment.

Restaurants need another mode

Reorder speed, pack sizes, GST data and support matter more than lifestyle storytelling.

Breadth needs curation

Variety, uses, recipes and cultivation prevent a broad catalogue from feeling generic.

Originality rule

References support transferable patterns only. Do not reproduce a competitor's wording, logo, photography, illustration or distinctive composition.

05 - Brand identity blueprint

Cultivated confidence, made actionable.

The brand should feel like a trusted grower, discerning food editor and reliable local supplier speaking with one voice.

Theme and vibe

Premium, authentic, grounded, sensory, precise, seasonal and quietly distinctive.

Visual keywords

harvest ledger, mineral paper, cultivated detail, precise abundance, quiet rule, tactile edge

Emotional objective

Make the customer feel informed, invited and confident that the product will justify a premium.

Brand promise

We show what is growing, what is ready and why it is worth bringing to your table.Working expression - not a locked tagline

Principles and anti-principles

Govern every touchpoint
Specific product names and varieties
Visible units, timing and delivery context
Verifiable cultivation language
Sensory copy with practical meaning
One obvious primary action
Status communicated in words

Do not

  • Use detox, miracle, guilt-free or superfood claims without evidence
  • Imply certification, organic status or health outcomes not documented
  • Use fake dew, hyper-saturation or anonymous farm imagery
  • Hide unavailable products without explaining the harvest state
  • Turn every surface into a rounded floating card
  • Use the deferred wordmark as if it were approved
Deferred identity work

Wordmark, logo, monogram and lockup direction remain outside this release. I1 Field Notes must remain usable without dependence on a final logo proportion.

06 - Tone of voice

V1 Cultivated Authority.

Precise, sensory, warm and transparent. Short confident headings pair with plain operational language.

Precise

Name the variety, pack, harvest state, source and expected timing.

Sensory

Describe flavour, texture and use without turning every line into poetry.

Transparent

Explain availability limits, substitutions and recovery clearly.

Sentence behavior

  • Headings: short, specific and active
  • Body: calm, direct and evidence-led
  • Product names: exact and scannable
  • CTAs: verb plus outcome
  • Error copy: state what happened, what remains safe and what to do next
  • Restaurant copy: operational without losing warmth

11 - Typography options

Editorial expression, operational clarity.

T2 Fraunces + DM Sans is canonical. Both options are open-license and self-hosted; pairings must never be mixed.

Selected T2
Cultivated for flavour.

Fraunces brings tactile contemporary character. DM Sans keeps product grids, forms and operational content compact and friendly.

  • Fraunces variable: display and H1-H3
  • DM Sans variable: body, UI and H4-H6
  • SIL Open Font License 1.1
  • Self-host WOFF2 with font-display: swap
  • Risk: excessive axes can feel trendy
Alternative T1
Harvest notes for the table.

Newsreader + Manrope is calmer and more literary. It was recommended initially but not selected.

  • Newsreader variable: display and H1-H3
  • Manrope variable: body and UI
  • SIL Open Font License 1.1
  • Risk: can become overly literary

Typography governance

Selected T2
Display restraint

Use moderate softness and stable axes. Do not vary Fraunces settings per component.

Readable measure

Paragraphs stop at 66ch; hero and product narratives stop at 54ch.

Loading

Preload roman display and body files; load italic only when used. Preserve OFL notices.

Fallbacks

Fraunces to Georgia to serif. DM Sans to Segoe UI to Arial to sans-serif.

Dense UI

Use DM Sans for tables, form labels, product names and admin navigation.

Minimum body

Body text never drops below 16px in implementation.

14 - Iconography and media

I1 Field Notes.

An original, crop-aware line system that adds cultivated character without compromising universal action recognition.

24px grid16, 20, 24 and 32px rendered sizes
1.75px strokeOptically consistent, not mechanically identical
Square capsRounded joins and open counters
Current colorSemantic meaning includes text

Construction behavior

  • Universal actions remain conventional
  • Living subjects may use controlled asymmetry
  • Icons reinforce labels rather than replace them
  • Icon-only controls require accessible names
  • Interactive icon targets remain at least 44x44px
  • New glyphs must follow the documented generator rules

Do not mix

  • Rounded monoline families
  • Bold bevelled commerce icons
  • Filled glyphs with unrelated geometry
  • Emoji as operational symbols
  • Logo or monogram concepts before the deferred identity checkpoint

Imagery direction

Production requires verified rights
Show

Texture, scale, hands, cultivation, harvest and finished-food context.

Avoid

Fake dew, hyper-saturation, heavy green grading and anonymous laboratory imagery.

Record

Photographer, source, permission, crop guidance and approved use.

Current specimen assets

The inherited product images hash-match existing WeGroween project files, but production-reuse rights were not independently verified. They remain specimen-only until permission is documented.

18 - Implementation and governance

Tokens first. Semantics always.

The system is framework-neutral today and can map to future semantic HTML, compiled Tailwind CSS, modular JavaScript, Supabase and Razorpay.

Developer handoff

  • Import `tokens.css` before component styles
  • Map Tailwind utilities to CSS variables; never duplicate palette hex values
  • Use `wg-` reference classes or equivalent semantic component APIs
  • Use data attributes for behavior, not styling hooks
  • Keep Supabase service-role and Razorpay secrets server-side
  • Verify payment signatures and process idempotent webhooks
  • Protect exposed tables with deliberate RLS policies and grants

Designer handoff

  • Use C2 and T2 as canonical
  • Keep G3 density while preserving editorial hierarchy
  • Extend I1 icons through the documented construction rules
  • Do not approve photography without source and rights records
  • Preserve explicit availability, delivery and recovery states
  • Do not revive rejected palette or type options without a new decision
  • Keep wordmark and logo work deferred
