# Our Story — illustration directions, round 2

PROPOSED. Separate review artifacts only. Production site and canonical DLS unchanged.

Recommendation: The Growing Still Life. The gouache treatment brings warmth and appetite while preserving Cultivated Editorial restraint.

## Living Lines

A drawing that feels observed, rather than assembled from symbols.

Fit: Fine botanical detail gives the growing story specificity. The light ink treatment leaves room for the existing editorial typography.

Discipline: Detailed stems and gills need simplifying at mobile size; avoid antique engraving and scientific specimen labels.

1. A loose climbing vegetable stem crosses a simple terrace planter; one cropped railing suggests home.
2. Microgreen stems and oyster mushroom fans are drawn with real organic differences.
3. A planted root line travels beyond its container and becomes the contour of a produce crate: the idea has outgrown home.
4. Keep the supplied logo, caption, crop motif and existing composition unchanged.
5. A few distinct vegetables, microgreens and mushrooms gathered around the rim of a quiet dinner plate.

Motion: One quiet 360ms reveal; later, a few separately drawn stems could resolve once as the chapter enters. Never animate the entire garden continuously.

## Layers of Growth

Small growing scenes become tactile, carefully constructed objects.

Fit: Layered leaves and mushroom caps echo the brand’s tactile character while bringing the greatest visual change from the current icon scenes.

Discipline: Keep the relief shallow and the shadows restrained; otherwise it can feel like a toy or a separate 3D brand.

1. One paper planter, a folded climbing stem and a barely raised terrace ledge.
2. Folded microgreen leaves and layered oyster mushroom fans share a shallow growing shelf.
3. An open paper produce crate expands around the tiny original planter, with one folded leaf extending past its edge.
4. Keep the supplied logo, caption, crop motif and existing composition unchanged.
5. A shallow cream paper plate receives sculpted produce; soft contact shadows supply depth.

Motion: A short 360ms reveal. A final layered version could settle its foreground by 4px once; no pointer tilt or permanent floating.

## The Growing Still Life

Warm, expressive still lifes make each chapter feel like an edition of the harvest.

Fit: A strong match for Cultivated Editorial: appetizing produce, tactile pigment and generous empty space, without competing with the logo.

Discipline: Brush texture must stay selective; full painted backgrounds or rustic props would make the page too busy.

1. One terracotta pot with a modest vegetable plant; a brush of light suggests the terrace.
2. A still life of microgreen trays and oyster mushroom fans, with a softly suggested indoor ledge.
3. An open harvest crate gathers the two crops; one small tray remains beside it to show the shift in scale.
4. Keep the supplied logo, caption, crop motif and existing composition unchanged.
5. A simple ceramic plate with a restrained arrangement of fresh produce, ready for the next chapter.

Motion: A single 360ms reveal of the completed illustration. If layered later, reveal just a small painted shadow; no looping plant growth.

Reduced motion: Show completed artwork immediately. No reveal, translation, parallax or automatic motion.

Provenance: Original symbolic illustrations generated with the built-in image_gen tool. They are not photographs or evidence of the historical garden. No outside artwork used.
