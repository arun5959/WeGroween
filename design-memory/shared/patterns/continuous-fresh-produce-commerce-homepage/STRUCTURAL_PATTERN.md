# Continuous fresh-produce commerce homepage

Status: reviewed-redacted structural pattern

## Appropriate use

Use this sequence when a fresh-produce or cultivation-led brand must serve household shoppers first while keeping a credible secondary route for restaurants or other recurring buyers. Revalidate all facts, rights, accessibility behavior and operational promises for the receiving project.

## Reusable sequence

1. Open with utility and service cues that reduce uncertainty before browsing.
2. Establish a catalogue-first hero with one primary household action and one quieter supply action.
3. Place a compact trust band immediately after the hero.
4. Show the current harvest with availability, unit and price visible on each card.
5. Check serviceability before the visitor builds a perishable basket.
6. Curate categories by kitchen use or buying intent rather than by taxonomy alone.
7. Add cultivation evidence close to shopping content.
8. Explain the delivery journey before checkout.
9. Present a household bundle or recurring edit as a convenience layer.
10. Give restaurants a distinct secondary pathway with a clear enquiry action.
11. Use editorial guidance to improve storage, preparation and product understanding.
12. Close with a harvest-focused action, essential policies and responsive interaction states.

## Interaction rules

- Keep household commerce visually primary and restaurant supply visibly available without making both pathways compete.
- Pair status words with markers; do not rely on color alone.
- Keep availability, pack or unit context and price readable when cards collapse on narrow screens.
- Preserve serviceability and delivery context near purchase actions.
- Use visible focus, useful error recovery and at least 44px touch targets.
- Label sample commercial data as illustrative until operations confirms it.

## Redaction boundary

This pattern contains no client name, identity assets, palette values, font selection, original copy, contact details, product catalogue, photography, pricing, service area, delivery promise or confidential operational detail. It is a structural starting point only, not a licensed template or evidence source.
